HOW TO PLAY

Move — left thumbstick (or WASD).

Attack — big ⚔ button (or J). Chains into a combo.

Jump ⇧ — clear gaps and dodge ground attacks (Space).

Dash ↯ — i-frames through danger (Shift).

Ruin ✕ — cleaving arc that hits everything in front (K).

Nova ◎ — radial burst, staggers everything nearby (L).

Bind ☗ — raise the fallen as Cinderbound soldiers who fight for you (U).

Clear every enemy to draw out the Anchor. Kill it to break the rift.

Level up to earn stat points. Cinderbound persist between rifts.
